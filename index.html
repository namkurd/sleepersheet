How these numbers work
Rumbles
Every week you earn 1 rumble for each team you outscore, plus 9 bonus rumbles if you also beat your head-to-head opponent. A perfect week is worth (teams in the league + 8): 18 in a 10-team league, 20 in a 12-team league.
Rumble %
Rumbles earned divided by the rumbles that were available over the games played.
Rumble R
Rank by rumbles. Rumbles became the official standings in 2022; earlier seasons show the rank each team would have had.
H2H W / L
Regular-season head-to-head record. Playoffs are not counted.
PF / PA
Points for and points against. PF R ranks most points first; PA R ranks fewest points against first.
H2H W%
Head-to-head wins divided by head-to-head games.
Field W% / Vs. Field
Your record if you had played every other team every week (each team you outscore is a win).
Luck +/-
H2H W% minus Field W%. Positive means you won more head-to-head games than your scoring earned; negative means you got unlucky.
▲1 / ▼1
How many spots that team's standing moved in Rumble R, H2H W, PF R, Field W% or Vs. Field since the last update (normally the prior week). No badge means no change since then.
Season slider
Drag either handle to pick a run of seasons and see their combined totals, or click a year underneath to see just that season. The full range is All-Time; “Reset to all years” brings it back.
Year breakdown
On All-Time or a multi-season view, hover (or tap) Rumbles, H2H W, H2H L, PF, PA or Vs. Field to see the year-by-year numbers behind the total.
